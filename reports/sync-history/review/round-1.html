  • domain: Search vs. To integration default: the state map doesn't record whether a transaction-ID search ignores the default To integration filter (Sandbox Company US 62ce). If it doesn't, syncs of the same transaction to the other accounting company (QB PT Demo - Shopify (Synder), 37 rows) are hidden, and the primary task ('all syncs of one transaction') could wrongly conclude the transaction never synced there.
