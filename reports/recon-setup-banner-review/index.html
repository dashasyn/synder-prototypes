Stripe logo

★Stripe logo (the feedback you asked for)Medium

UX ×3 · Domain · Clarity

WhyIn B a single Stripe card is fine, and even helps: one card shows 'one integration' better than the copy does. Risk is mild: a Shopify or PayPal user may wonder 'is this Stripe-only?' for a second. Clarity rated it below the quit-risk threshold. Domain: no accounting risk. In A the problem isn't Stripe. Variant B · one line

1The headline doesn't say 'one integration'High

All 5 validators

Why'We compare your integration data with your books' reads as 'all my integrations'. The one-integration rule appears nowhere on screen.

Fix'We compare one integration's data with your books.'

2'We pull' promises automation, but the form offers upload methodsMedium

All 5 validators

Why'pull' is a banned term (vocabulary.md → Import). It also implies the system fetches everything, while Assisted and Manual mean the user uploads files.

Fix'We import transactions from both sides, match them, and show you…'

3Two green success signals before any runMedium

UX ×2

WhyThe green ✓ chip plus the green text read as 'already matched / all good' on a setup screen.

FixNeutral compare mark (⇄) between the cards; the read-only line in body grey or kept green, but not both green.

4'what's missing or doesn't add up' is vagueMedium

Domain

WhyIt doesn't say which side the transaction is missing from, and it drops duplicates, which the product actually checks.

FixOptional: name the checks, e.g. '…missing from your books, mismatched amounts or duplicated.'
