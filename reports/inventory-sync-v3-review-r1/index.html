Inventory sync v3 — validator round 1

Target projects/inventory-sync-v3 @ f7a3121 · UX ×3, Accountant, Business owner (Fidelity: no spec; Trust, A11Y: not requested) · state map 9 states × both layouts, gate PASS · verify PASS · 16 findings → 7 themes

Verdict: the setup works and every step has one clear action. The two strongest problems are the ends of loops: a fixed held change is never prompted to send, and a ready store with sync off looks finished.
HighUX ×3 (corroborated)

1. A held change stays held after the user fixes its cause

Why: After 'Match it' → matching the item, the Events row still says 'Item not matched', Held stays 1 and Mappings says 'Every product is matched.' Nothing says Retry is still needed, so the +12 never reaches Shopify.

Fix: After a mapping that fixes a held change: toast '1 held change can be sent now · Retry'; the Events row reads 'Matched · ready to send' with an inline Retry.

HighUX ×3 (corroborated)

2. 'Ready' and a vanished banner read as 'done' while sync is still off

Why: When location + setting are done the banner disappears and the badge says Ready; Enable sits in another column (KF-2 pattern).

Fix: While sync is off and a store is ready: banner 'My ceramic plates is ready. Enable sync to start sending stock changes.' with Enable inside; badge 'Ready · sync off'.

HighAccountant + Business owner

3. 'Created product should be: Inventory' never says what it changes

Why: Switching to Inventory makes products Synder creates in QuickBooks Inventory items, whose sales post Cost of Goods Sold and use an Inventory Asset account. Users can't tell what happens to their books.

Fix: Keep the exact setting name; add one helper line: 'Products Synder creates in QuickBooks will be Inventory items, so their sales post Cost of Goods Sold.' + Learn more. (Needs product to confirm existing items are untouched.)

HighBusiness owner + Accountant

4. Two sentences that mislead about what sync does

Why: 'Only changes made after you enable are sent' doesn't say existing differences stay; 'won't sync' means 'won't reach the books' in Synder's own vocabulary.

Fix: 'Stock that already differs stays as it is — see Stock check.' and '1 product has no QuickBooks item, so its stock changes won't be sent to Shopify.'

MediumAccountant + Business owner

5. Accept QuickBooks dialog promises a total but sends a difference

Why: 'will be set to 140 (change +8)' — the +8 is from the last comparison; if stock moved since, Shopify won't end at 140. 'on hand', 'sync event' are jargon.

Fix: 'Synder sends +8 to Shopify so Ceramic plate 24cm goes from 132 to 140 (difference at the last comparison, 02:30 UTC). Compare now first if stock moved since.'

MediumUX ×2

6. 'Connect store' is the strongest button during setup

Why: It is the only filled button while the required fixes are small outlined ones, and later it competes with Enable.

Fix: Outlined 'Connect store' once a store exists.

MediumUX ×1 each

7. Stock check count ≠ Reconcile list; Ignore has no undo

Why: Block says 2 (stores with a location) but Reconcile lists 3 (includes the store without a location). Ignore acts at once with no way back.

Fix: Reconcile skips stores without a location too; add Undo to Ignore (toast + row).
