Inventory sync — UX review, round 1

Target: projects/inventory-sync @ 26106a1 · UX validator ×3 (Domain and Clarity not run, per Ignat) · state map: 36 controls, 11 states, real Chromium · gate PASS · verify PASS

Verdict: the setup walk works end to end with one open step at a time. The problems are after setup: the page says setup is complete when it isn't, and a store added later has no way to finish its setup from the Overview.
High3/3 UX runs

1. 'Setup · 5 of 5 done' claims completion when a new store re-opened step 2 or step 4 was skipped

Why it matters: Users are told the job is finished when it is not (KF-2 drop-off pattern), so stock differences and misconfigured stores stay unfixed.

Reproduce: Switch to '2 · Sync running', use '+ Connect another Shopify store' to add third-shop, pick Warehouse location, read the summary, then click Review steps
Observed: Collapsed line reads 'Setup · 5 of 5 done'; expanded setup reads 'Step 2 of 5 · 4 done' with step 2 open ('third-shop (Shopify) … Non-inventory · Needs change') and step 4 'Skipped. 10 products still differ from QuickBooks'

Fix: Compute from real step state; e.g. 'Setup · 4 of 5 done · 1 needs attention'; Review steps opens the unfinished step

High3/3 UX runs

2. Attention line 'creates Non-inventory items · Open Settings' is a dead end — no path, no Check again

Why it matters: A user who fixes the setting and returns still sees the warning and cannot clear it, so they loop back to Settings or assume the change did not work.

Reproduce: In '2 · Sync running', add third-shop, pick a location, click the attention line's Open Settings, then click Run now
Observed: Toast 'Settings opened in a new tab.'; no Check again appears; after Run now the line 'third-shop (Shopify) creates Non-inventory items · Open Settings' is still present unchanged

Fix: Same numbered path + Check again as step 2, inline or by opening step 2

Medium3/3 UX runs

3. Ignore does not survive the next Compare now (same as production today)

Why it matters: Users who deliberately accept a difference must re-ignore it after every comparison, and the Overview's 'products differ' count creeps back up without explanation.

Reproduce: Compare stock: click Ignore on a row (Stone 13 / Stone 1), return to Overview, then click Compare now
Observed: Row became 'Ignored · –' and attention fell from 10 to 9 products differ; after Compare now the state map shows 'Stone 13 … Unresolved · Set Shopify to QuickBooks Ignore' again

Fix: Keep Ignored until quantities change; exclude from attention count; Undo

Medium2/3 UX runs

4. Check again hidden until Open Settings is clicked

Why it matters: Users who fixed the setting beforehand, or via an already-open Settings tab, see no way forward from step 2 and may think they are stuck.

Reproduce: Open step 2 and look for Check again before clicking Open Settings, then click Open Settings
Observed: Before: Check again present=false, only 'Open Settings' action; after click: toast 'Settings opened in a new tab.' and Check again appears

Fix: Show Check again from the start

Medium1/3 UX runs

5. Compare stock empty state during setup says 'Run one with Compare now' but there is no button

Why it matters: A user who opens Compare stock during setup to check stock differences hits a dead end: the instruction points to a control that isn't there and nothing says the tab only works after setup.

Reproduce: Open the prototype (1 · First setup) and click the Compare stock tab, at step 1 and again after reaching step 3
Observed: The tab shows only 'No comparisons yet. Run one with Compare now.' and the only visible buttons are the variant switcher and the four tabs; Compare now appears only after Turn on sync

Fix: During setup: say comparisons come in step 4 / after turning on, link to setup

Medium1/3 UX runs

6. Per-row 'Set Shopify to QuickBooks' overwrites stock with no confirm (bulk and production both confirm)

Why it matters: A user who learned in setup that this action is confirmed can overwrite live Shopify stock with one click in Compare stock, including on rows where QuickBooks has negative quantities.

Reproduce: Click 'Set Shopify to QuickBooks numbers (8)' in step 4, then in Compare stock tab click 'Set Shopify to QuickBooks' on Stone 12
Observed: Step 4 opens modal 'Set Shopify stock to QuickBooks?' with Cancel; in Compare stock, Stone 12 immediately becomes Shopify 137 / 'Set to QuickBooks' with no confirmation.

Fix: Reuse the confirm dialog (production had one: 'Accept QuickBooks quantity')
