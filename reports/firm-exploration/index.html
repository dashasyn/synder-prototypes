Firms in Synder: userflow exploration

Live walk on demo.synderapp.com, 29 Oct 2026 · firm created from Bot Test Company · clients DS_test_2_dasha and Dasha Test Company · Professional accountant validator: 2 rounds, harness PASS

Verdict. The firm-side screens are richer than expected: a per-client month-close workspace with checklists, notes, tags and a timeline. The connection flow around them is the weak part. Clients are connected by passing a 10-digit Firm ID outside Synder. Neither side gets notified of anything. The client can't disconnect. Removing a client silently destroys the firm's month-close history.

Simplest part: creating the firm (one field, 3 clicks). Hardest part: connecting a client, which takes two people, two products and an out-of-band step with no feedback.

1 · The flow as it works today

  1. Firm admin Org settings → Users card → Accounting firm tab → Create a Firm link → landing → modal with Firm's name * → Done! → Go to My Firm.
  2. Firm admin Clients → Add client. This doesn't add anything. It's an instruction modal: copy the Firm ID, Send it to your client any way you prefer, ask them to enter it in My account --> Accounting firm tab.
  3. Client owner must find Org settings → Users card → Accounting firm tab (the real path; "My account" doesn't exist), paste the ID and click Apply. Status Pending. No confirmation is shown.
  4. Firm admin isn't notified (bell stays 0). They have to open Clients to find the Pending row → Approve (one click, no confirm) → Connected.
  5. Firm admin Colleagues → Add colleague → Name, Email, one client (required) and its role (Manager/Member). An email invite is sent and the colleague shows as Invited.
  6. Firm team works each client in Client details: Work (month status, firm checks, notes, Synder evidence, timeline) · Billing · Firm colleagues.
  7. Firm admin getting back to the firm: avatar menu → Firm. That's the only entry point. The org switcher has none.

2 · What was simple, what wasn't

Simple

  • Creating a firm: one required field, inline Required, clear success modal.
  • Entering the Firm ID: whitespace is trimmed, one-firm-at-a-time rule is stated.
  • Approve is one click; the list row updates in place (Open month, Checks, Status).
  • Client details form: good accountant fields (entity type, FYE, close deadline, services), email validation.
  • Firm checklist: suggested checks, "Required to close", applies to all open months, unsaved-changes guard.
  • Ticking a check stamps Done by Dasha, Oct 29, 2026, 7:07 PM UTC, which is a real audit trail.
  • Search by client name works.

Not simple

  • Connecting a client depends on an ID passed outside Synder, with stale instructions and no notification on either side.
  • The client-side entry point is a second tab inside the Users card. Nothing there reads as "accountant".
  • The firm has no home. It renders inside whichever org is current (that org's sidebar and trial banner) and is reachable only from the avatar menu.
  • "Connect client" in the org switcher creates a new org through onboarding and never mentions the firm.
  • Inviting a colleague forces you to pick exactly one client. You can't choose a firm role, and Manager vs Member is never explained.
  • Removing a client destroys its month history, and the confirmation doesn't say so.
  • Billing: the firm tab is just "Book a call"; the client Billing tab says "No data".
