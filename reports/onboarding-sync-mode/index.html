Choose how Synder organizes your transactions

Synder can record every transaction individually, or group them into summary entries.

Recommended

Every order, refund and fee is recorded as its own entry.

  • checkEach order is a separate entry, under the customer's name.
  • checkYou can search your books by customer, order number or product.
  • checkSales tax is recorded order by order, by tax code — down to county or city.

info Your books grow with your order volume — one entry per sale, refund and fee.

Your sales are grouped into one summarized entry — daily, or per payout.

  • checkOne entry per day or per payout, however many orders it covers.
  • checkTotals are split into income, shipping, tax, discounts and fees.

info Customer and order names stay on each transaction in Synder — summary entries show totals only.
