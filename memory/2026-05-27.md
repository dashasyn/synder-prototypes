## Morning Idea — UX Pattern from Another Product
- **Topic:** Notion/Figma-style breadcrumb navigation in GDV v3
- **Angle:** UX pattern from another product
- **Idea:** The GDV hierarchy is 4 levels deep (Schedules → Route → Message Group → Individual Message). Once editing a specific message, there's no ambient context. Notion's sticky breadcrumb solves this: `Schedules / Route 7 / Morning Announcements / Doors Closing` at the top of every deep page. ~10 min: static `<nav aria-label="breadcrumb">` with `/` separators using data already in the URL or page heading. Pairs with May 17's route-context filter — breadcrumb shows `Route 7 (filtered)` inline. Prevents "which route am I editing?" confusion mid-review with transit operators.
