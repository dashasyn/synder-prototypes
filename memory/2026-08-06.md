## Clarification — "Unpaid invoices only" copy is ambiguous (2026-08-05 19:53 UTC)

Ignat pushed back: selectors are NOT locked — user can pick any option, but the rule only applies
it to unpaid invoices; paid invoices can't be changed. My earlier "Unpaid invoices only" copy was
flagged by him as unclear, and I agreed: it reads as a *permission* restriction ("only choose this
for unpaid invoices") when the real meaning is an *effect* restriction ("pick anything, it just
won't touch paid invoices").

**Revised recommendation:**
> Not applied to paid invoices

**Per-field variant, if unambiguous is worth 3 strings instead of 1:**
> Paid invoices keep their current item
> Paid invoices keep their current tax
> Paid invoices keep their current amounts

Avoid: "Unpaid invoices only" / "Can only be applied to unpaid invoices" — both describe a
restriction on the *control*, but the control isn't restricted. Still grey helper text, not amber
— nothing is wrong when the form opens.

**Reassessed the top-vs-bottom framing given this new info**: by my own earlier rule (control
behavior → inline, rule outcome/scope → banner), this fact is actually rule-scope, which argues
for a banner. Inline still wins in practice, though, because only 3 of ~10 fields behave this
way — a banner alone forces the user to hold "which fields" in their head.

**Bigger gap flagged, not yet actioned**: the real risk is post-run, not pre-run — a user could
save a rule, see it report success, and have paid invoices silently left untouched. Recommended
a run-result message surfacing this explicitly, e.g. "Updated 12 invoices. Item and tax weren't
changed on 4 paid invoices." Framed as higher-value than the inline helper text alone.

## Follow-up review — updated banner design (2026-08-05 19:57 UTC)

Ignat sent a revised screenshot (warning icons dropped, banner now blue). Review:

**Real bug found**: banner text — "You can set Item, Tax Rate, and Amounts here, but changes will
be applied to unpaid invoices" — is missing the word **"only"**. As written, the "but" clause
reads as reassurance, not a caveat; a fast reader concludes nothing is restricted. Needs:
"...applied **only** to unpaid invoices," or better, cut the "you can set" preamble entirely:
> Item, tax, and amounts are updated on unpaid invoices only. Paid invoices keep their current
> values.

**Redundancy**: banner and inline text now say nearly the same thing twice — worse than before,
not better. Recommended picking one; leaning inline since only 3 of 6 line fields are affected
and inline is what tells the user which ones.

**Field-name mismatch persists**: banner quotes "Tax Rate" (actual label: "Tax") and "Amounts"
(actual label: "Amounts are"). If banner is kept, quote labels exactly or don't quote at all.

**Color now contradicts severity logic**: blue banner (informational) vs amber inline (caution)
for the identical fact — inconsistent signal for a non-error state. Recommended both neutral
(grey inline, blue banner if kept), since nothing is broken when the form opens.

**Inline copy refined further**: "Only applies to unpaid invoices" still requires the reader to
infer "so paid ones won't change." Recommended stating the surprising half directly:
> Paid invoices aren't changed
Shorter too — may avoid wrapping to two lines under each of three stacked fields.

**Still outstanding, unresolved across all rounds**: dev-string leakage ("line custom", "Tax,
item, amounts are", "line4"), "Submit" → should read "Update invoice", filled dark-grey "Close"
button competing with primary action, Title Case modal title vs sentence-case style guide,
"Please note that" filler. None actioned by Ignat yet as of this log.
