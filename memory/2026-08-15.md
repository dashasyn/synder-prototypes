## Morning idea (2026-08-15) — name the reset controls by scope, not by verb

Took the smallest unclosed item on the Filtering Options list: *"two reset labels should
become one"*, open since 2026-08-06 and still phrased as a to-do rather than a proposal.

The real problem isn't duplication — it's that both controls are named by **verb**
(reset / clear) when what a user needs to know is **scope**: what exactly disappears when I
click this.

Proposal: name each by what it clears.
- Panel-level control → **"Clear filters"**
- Row-level control (also drops the active segment/preset) → **"Clear all"**

One verb throughout (*clear*, never *reset* — matches `vocabulary.md`'s preference for the
plain user-facing verb), scope carried by the noun, and the pair reads as a hierarchy instead
of two synonyms competing. If only one is ever visible at a time, collapse to "Clear all" and
drop the other entirely.

~2 min of copy, closes a listed open item, needs one yes/no from Ignat rather than a product
decision.
