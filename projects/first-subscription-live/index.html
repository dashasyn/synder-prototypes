FAQ
What transactions am I charged for?
All plans are functionality based and Synder charges you for the synchronization of any transaction types (sales receipts, refunds, payouts, etc.) except processing fees. Processing fees will not affect your monthly sync balance in Synder. If you need additional services like invoicing, smart rules, etc., please purchase them additionally on the ‘Organization settings → Manage subscription’ page. You can find the pricing details in “Support“ and “Add-ons“ sections.
Why can't I change currency when resubscribing?
Synder allows payments in several currencies. If your existing plan is in one currency, and you wish to change it to another one this feature will not be available until the end of the current paid period. Once the billing cycle is over, you will see the switcher and will be able to subscribe with another currency.
Will a discount remain if I update the plan?
Yes, the discount(s) you have will automatically be applied for your new plan. You can even change the credit card, it will not affect the discounts.
How can I update my subscription?
You can do so at any time in your Synder account. During the update if there is something left on your subscription balance the system will know that and the charge will be reduced automatically.
Can I get a refund for the subscription?
According to our Terms and Conditions once the purchase is made, it is final and non refundable. However, in case of any questions, please reach out to our support team. They will try their utmost to assist you!
Are there any discounts available for me?
We appreciate your loyalty greatly, and in your account you will be able to see the discounts you unlock for particular periods of usage. The longer you are with us, the cheaper the solution is for you!
Is tax included in the subscription price?
Taxes (e.g., VAT/GST/sales tax) may be charged on top of the subscription price based on your billing address and local regulations. The exact tax amount is calculated at checkout and itemized on your invoices.
