Key Capabilities

Multi-channel, real-time, always accurate

🖥️

Platform Displays

LED and LCD departure boards with real-time data feeds. Handles delays, track changes, and cancellations the moment they're confirmed in dispatch.

📺

In-Vehicle Screens

Onboard passenger information systems showing next stops, connections, and delay status — continuously updated from the control center.

📢

Automated Announcements

Text-to-speech and pre-recorded announcements triggered automatically by operational events. Multilingual support.

📱

Digital Channel API

Push real-time data to your app, website, or journey planner. Standard GTFS-RT and SIRI feeds available.

♿

Accessibility Compliance

Audio announcements, high-contrast displays, and structured data feeds that comply with EU PRM and national accessibility requirements.

🔔

Disruption Propagation

When a disruption hits one line, affected connections across the network are automatically updated — no manual intervention required.
