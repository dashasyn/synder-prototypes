    QuickBooks Online · Disconnected 2 days ago
    Critical ▾
    Your QuickBooks Online account has been disconnected. Synder cannot post transactions to your books until you reconnect. All syncs are currently paused.
    How to fix
    1 Go to Settings → Integrations → QuickBooks Online
    2 Click "Reconnect" and sign in with your QBO credentials
    3 Confirm permissions — Synder needs read/write access to transactions
    4 Synder will automatically resume syncing pending transactions
