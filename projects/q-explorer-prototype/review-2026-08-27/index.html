Q-Explorer — prototype review

QMS RPV CH · projects/q-explorer-prototype/index.html · Rounds 1 & 2, both verified · 27 August 2026

Status — all findings applied except one

35 of the 37 findings are fixed in the prototype (commits 7a4c0c1…489ac3b). Every fix was verified in a real browser; the resolved list lives in findings-log.json.

Still open (1): the nine finished evaluations that cannot be opened. Rohdaten Export, Linienanalyse DPM and Datenqualitätsindex are getting their own report views — waiting on reference screenshots.

Withdrawn as false positives (2):

  • “37 translation keys missing from both dictionaries” — wrong. The extraction regex only matched one-key-per-line entries; a robust count finds zero missing. The real defect was 15 <option> elements carrying no data-i18n attribute at all, which is why five breakdown options stayed German in EN mode. Fixed.
  • “Step-3 summary runs the filter values together” — wrong. The chips are a flex row with a 5px gap and never overlapped; the reading came from a textContent measurement rather than the rendered page.

Both were measurement artifacts on my side, not prototype defects. Logged in findings-log.json under withdrawn.

Verdict

The structure is easy to understand. What it shows you cannot be trusted, and the controls that would let you change what you are looking at are mostly not wired up. Someone sat in front of this tool works out what it is for without help — the landing list, the three-step wizard and the report layout all read correctly, and the wizard’s live auto-naming is genuinely good design. Comprehension is not the problem.

Two rounds, twelve independent validator passes, 52 findings. They split cleanly into two different faults:

Creating an evaluation is understandable but lossy. Three ways to lose or fail to reach your work (C1, C2, C3), and no keyboard route into the tool at all (C5).

Reading a report is the reverse — it looks finished and misstates its own contents. The main analysis control does nothing (C4). A Total row aggregates a population 13.8× larger than the rows under it with no disclosure (H1). Two evaluations with different periods and different scopes render the identical table (H2). One row’s list period and report period disagree by a month (H3), and every report title contradicts its own date chip (H4).

The single sentence version: the parts that show you numbers look trustworthy and are not; the parts that let you interrogate the numbers are decorative. Roughly 50 inputs, selects and buttons carry no handler at all.

Method

Followed personas/VALIDATOR_PROTOCOL.md v2. Recon was a real Chromium pass (Playwright) over all 13 views in German and English, split into two flows, each with its own state map, manifest and six independent validators run blind to each other. Both state maps passed validator-check.js statemap, so every panel-opening control has a recorded commit path (open → pick → reach Apply → repeat) rather than just “it opens”.

Critical

C1

Clicking the middle of a filled filter wipes it

Critical
Wizard step 2 — all six filter controls (Kantone, TU’s, RPV, Verkehrsmittel, Linien, Haltestellen) · UX ×3 + Trust — 4 independent lenses, conf 95

.custom-select-trigger lays out three children — label, clear-✕, caret — with justify-content: space-between. That puts the ✕ at the horizontal centre of the control. Measured: the trigger is 542px wide, the ✕ occupies x=769–789, and the centre is x=789. document.elementFromPoint(centre) returns canton-clear.

ReproducedPicked Kanton AG (state ['AG'], label “AG”). Closed the panel. Clicked the trigger to reopen it → panel did not open, state became [], label reverted to “All cantons”. Same on TU’s: ['BLS'] → []. No message, no undo.

Why it matters. An analyst reopening a filter to check or extend it destroys the scope instead, then runs the evaluation across every canton believing it is still restricted.

Fix. Put the ✕ and the caret in one right-aligned group (or give the label flex:1) so the centre of the control always opens the panel. Shrink the ✕ hit area to its glyph and add a “Cleared — undo” toast.

C2

Editing a saved schedule shows a blank configuration under its name

Critical
Geplante Berichte → Edit · UX ×3 + Trust

The wizard opens titled “Fahrtausfälle DPM – Wöchentlich” with a “Scheduled” badge, but none of the saved configuration is loaded, and the schedule section is hidden (display:none) so the frequency cannot be seen or changed.

ReproducedClicked Edit on the weekly Fahrtausfälle schedule. Read state: selectedPeriod: 'cur_month', selectedTU: [], selectedCantons: [], selectedLines: [] — the defaults, not the schedule.

Why it matters. The screen asserts it is one specific schedule while showing something else. A user changing one canton can replace a scoped BAV report with an unscoped default, or simply misread what is being measured.

Fix. Hydrate the wizard from the stored schedule and keep the schedule section visible in edit mode. If hydration isn’t built yet, title the screen “New evaluation from schedule” instead of the schedule’s name.

C3

Nine of the nineteen finished evaluations cannot be opened at all

Critical
Evaluations list — every Rohdaten Export, Linienanalyse DPM and Datenqualitätsindex row, plus two Fahrtausfälle rows · UX ×3 round 1 + UX ×3 round 2 + Clarity, conf 90

19 rows show status Fertig. Ten expose ['Anzeigen','delete']; nine expose only ['delete'], and the row name is not clickable either (onclick absent, cursor:auto). No tooltip, no empty state, no explanation.

ReproducedRow by row from the DOM. The nine: Rohdaten Export – Gestern / – Letzte Woche / – April 2026 komplett; Linienanalyse DPM – Letztes Jahr, Kanton BE / – Letzter Monat, S 1, S 5 / – Aktueller Monat, RE 7, BLS; Datenqualitätsindex DPM – Letzter Monat, alle TU / – Letztes Jahr, SBB, BLS, ZVV; and Fahrtausfälle DPM – Letzter Monat, BLS, Kanton BE and – Letztes Jahr, Bahn Normalspur, which sit two rows below Fahrtausfälle entries that do have Anzeigen. Same type, same status, different actions.

Why it matters. The first step of reading a report dead-ends on half the finished evaluations. The status says the work succeeded and the only offered action is delete.

Fix. Give every Fertig row an open path (clickable name + Anzeigen). Where a type genuinely has no viewable output, say so in the row — a disabled action with “Ergebnis nur als Download” / “Keine Berichtsansicht für diesen Typ”.

C4

“Aufschlüsseln” — the main analysis control — does nothing on any report

Critical
Anschlusspünktlichkeit “Aufschlüsseln nach” · Pünktlichkeit and Fahrtausfälle “Aufschlüsseln” · UX ×3 round 2, all Critical, conf 95 · + deterministic pass

The three-level breakdown is what makes a DPM report a report rather than a single number. On V5 the three selects (#rpt-aufschluss-1/-2/-3) have no onchange attribute at all. On V8 and V11 the selects do have a handler, but it only drives the option-disabling cascade — never the table. The cascade is the trap: greying out the dimension already used one level up is visible feedback, so the control feels alive while the numbers stay grouped the old way.

ReproducedV11: switched level 1 Linienbündel → Haltestelle → TU konzessioniert → Monat. Table signature captured after each — byte-identical every time (20 rows, first column header still “Name / Linienbündel”, same first row, same Total). Setting all three levels changed nothing. V5: level 1 to “TU (connector)”, then “Line (connector)” — 30 rows unchanged. No toast, no message, no loading state.

Why it matters. Reading a DPM report means slicing it. The user picks a dimension, sees the cascade react, reads the same table, and concludes the network looks the same by stop as by line bundle.

Fix. Wire the selects to re-render and re-aggregate the table, and relabel the first column to the chosen dimension. Until then, disable them.

C5

There is no keyboard route into the tool

Critical
Type-selection screen, wizard step 2, sidebar nav · A11Y round 1 — 3 Critical findings, conf 95–98

Every entry point is a <div onclick> with no role and no tabindex:

  • The six report-type cards. 40 Tab presses from a blurred document: 11 stops, all page chrome (topbar ×5, language select, Log out, the four footer links), then it wraps. Not one card. .focus() called directly on a card also fails — tabIndex is −1.
  • All six filter dropdowns in step 2. 45 Tab presses: the whole cycle is chrome → Back → Back → Next → wrap. No filter trigger appears at all. None has tabindex, role, aria-expanded or aria-haspopup.
  • The sidebar nav items. 53 presses, 18 unique stops, not one div.nav-item. Scheduled Reports is only reachable by mouse, even though everything inside it is keyboard-operable.

Why it matters. This is a BAV-facing reporting tool. A keyboard-only or screen-reader user cannot start an evaluation, cannot set a single filter, and cannot reach the scheduled-reports screen. Not degraded — impossible.

Fix. Real <button>s for the type cards (accessible name = title + description). role="combobox" + tabindex="0" + aria-expanded + aria-haspopup="listbox" on the filter triggers, with Enter/Space/Arrows to open and Escape to close. <nav> with real buttons or links and aria-current for the sidebar.

C6

The chart popup is not a modal — focus stays behind it and Escape does nothing

Critical
Pünktlichkeit chart overlay #punct-chart-overlay · A11Y round 2, Critical, conf 95

A plain div with no role and no aria-modal that never receives focus, never contains it, and never closes on Escape.

ReproducedEnter on the row’s Chart button → overlay opens, focus stays on the trigger behind it. The next 8 Tabs all land on background table buttons the overlay is covering. 41 Tab presses to reach the overlay’s own first control. Escape leaves it open (display:flex unchanged) — only × or an outside click dismisses it.

Why it matters. Opening a chart is an explicit step of the read-a-report task. A keyboard user lands behind the thing they just opened, tabs blind through 40 hidden buttons, and cannot dismiss it. The Info modals added this week do handle Escape — so the app is inconsistent with itself.

Fix. role="dialog" aria-modal="true" plus an accessible name, move focus to the heading or close button on open, trap Tab/Shift+Tab, close on Escape, return focus to the row’s Chart button.

High — the report lies about its own contents

These four came from the Trust and Clarity lenses in round 2 and are new since the interim report. They are the reason the verdict changed from “unfinished” to “untrustworthy”.

H1

The Total row sums a population 13.8× larger than the rows beneath it, undisclosed

High
Pünktlichkeit report — tr.punct-row-gesamt over 19 displayed Linienbündel rows · Trust round 2, conf 88

The table shows 19 bundle rows under a Total. The Total is not their sum — it is the whole network. Nothing on the screen says the list is truncated.

ReproducedSummed the 19 tr.punct-row-bundle rows column by column: Soll 152 727 / Ist 149 078. Total row: 2 101 488 / 2 048 996 / 1 861 401 / 52 492 / 90,84% — 13.8× the visible rows. Paginator present: false. Search for “of N” / “von N” / “weitere”: []. The chips on the same screen read “37 TU’s”, “17 Kantone”, “All lines” while 15 distinct TUs are displayed.

Why it matters. An analyst computing a TU’s share of the network gets ARAG at 15 854 / 2 101 488 = 0,75% instead of roughly 10%. Or reads “no TU after BBA has a punctuality problem” off a list that stops at B. Either goes to the BAV as a materially wrong figure, and the screen gives no reason to doubt it.

Fix. Paginate with an explicit “19 von 37 Transportunternehmen”, or reuse the prototype’s own truncation pattern — the “+ 29 weitere Linien” row already used in the chip dropdowns — as the last table row. Label the aggregate “Total (alle 37 TU)”.

H2

Two evaluations with different periods and different scopes render the identical table

High
Pünktlichkeit report — params chips vs. table body · Trust round 2, conf 78
ReproducedOpened “Pünktlichkeit – Letzter Monat, Kanton ZH”: period 01.04.2026–30.04.2026, Total 2.101.488 / 2.048.996 / 1.861.401 / 52.492 / 90,84%, 26 rows. Returned to the list, opened “Pünktlichkeit – Letztes Jahr, alle Kantone”: period 01.01.2025–31.12.2025, Total identical to the digit, 26 rows. One month of one canton and a full calendar year of the whole country produce the same 2 101 488 scheduled trips.

Why it matters. Because the figures never move when the stated scope does, there is no way for a user to notice a mislabelled report — which is exactly what H3 produces. Someone signs off an annual 2025 figure that is in fact an April 2026 table.

Fix. Derive the table from the evaluation’s period and scope. While there is no backend, mark the figures explicitly — a “Demodaten” badge next to the params chips — so they cannot be read as period-specific.

H3

One evaluation states two different periods, a month apart

High
Fahrtausfälle DPM – Letzte 7 Tage, SBB — list PERIOD cell vs. #fa-p-von/#fa-p-bis · Trust round 2, conf 90
ReproducedList row PERIOD cell: 19.05–25.05.2026. The same row’s attributes: von=10.06.2026 bis=23.06.2026. Opened report header: 10.06.2026 – 23.06.2026. The other eight openable rows match, so this row is the one that diverges — but the hardcoded fallback in showFAReport (index.html:7239-7240) would mislabel any row whose attributes are missing. No report view shows a generation timestamp to arbitrate.

Why it matters. An analyst who picked this evaluation because the list said 19.05–25.05 attributes 33 483 cancelled trips to the wrong two weeks — and reports a May cancellation rate to the BAV using June data.

Fix. Render the list’s PERIOD cell from the same data-von/data-bis the report reads, so the two cannot diverge. Replace the hardcoded date fallback with an explicit “Zeitraum unbekannt”.

H4

Every report title contradicts the date chip directly beneath it

High
All three report views · Clarity round 2, conf 88

“Pünktlichkeit – Letzte 30 Tage, Tarifverbund A” sits directly above the chip “26.04.2026 – 25.05.2026”. Same on V5 (“Anschlusspünktlichkeit – Letzte 30 Tage” over “01.04.2026 – 30.04.2026”) and V8. Two answers to “what period is this?” on one screen.

ReproducedV11 renders a reconciling subtitle — Auswertung: Punctuality DPM (25.06.2026 22:54:42) in #punct-eval-sub — but V5 and V8 have none at all, so on those two there is nothing to resolve the contradiction with. (The state map recorded all three as blank; it queried .page-subtitle, a class those headers never use. Corrected here.)

Fix. Render the period from the stored evaluation window (“Pünktlichkeit · 26.04.2026–25.05.2026, Tarifverbund A”), keep any user-given name as a secondary line, and put V11’s “Auswertung … (timestamp)” subtitle on V5 and V8 too.

Every raw-data column filter is decorative

Pünktlichkeitsdaten (16 inputs + 3 selects) · Rohdaten Anschluss (11 inputs) · Deterministic pass + recon

H6

The first click on a bundle row does nothing, and the caret never changes

High
Pünktlichkeit report — button.punct-expand-btn · UX ×3 round 2 + Trust, conf 92–95

The button renders without the collapsed class while its line rows render already hidden, so the first click re-hides hidden rows and only flips the flag. The icon is expand_more in every state and aria-expanded is null.

ReproducedFresh load, four clicks with a probe after each: 20/26 visible, icon expand_more, no .collapsed → click 1: 20/26 unchanged, class gains .collapsed, icon unchanged → click 2: 26/26 → click 3: 20/26 → click 4: 26/26. The button reported isVisible() && isEnabled() throughout — the first click really is absorbed.

H7

The param chips look like the report’s filters and are read-only

High
Chips “37 TU’s ▾”, “17 Kantone ▾”, “42 Linien ▾” on V5/V8/V11 · UX ×2 round 2 + Clarity, conf 80–85

Caret, pointer cursor, a dropdown that opens and lists TU names — every affordance of an editable multi-select.

ReproducedOpened the “15 TU’s” chip on V5: panel lists AAGL, AAGR, AB, ABI, … Every .rpt-chip-dd-item has cursor:auto and onclick=false. Zero buttons inside the dropdown, zero Apply/Anwenden/Übernehmen anywhere in the view. A second click just collapses it.

Why it matters. To change one canton you have to abandon the report and rebuild the whole evaluation in the wizard — and nothing on the screen tells you that.

Fix. Either drop the caret and pointer cursor and present them as a plain read-only “Parameter dieser Auswertung” summary, or keep the panel and add the route out: “Umfang fixiert — Parameter ändern → neue Auswertung”.

H8

The punctuality report never says what counts as good

High
V11 — no KPI cards, column header “Wert”, chart legend “Value” · UX ×3 round 2 + Clarity, conf 80–85

Zero KPI cards, against 4 on Anschlusspünktlichkeit (each labelled “Threshold: 90%”) and 3 on Fahrtausfälle. The only summary figure is 90,84% sitting in the Total row of a 20-row table, in a column headed just “Wert”, with no pass/fail colouring. The chart legend reads “Value” with no reference line — while the Fahrtausfälle chart draws its 3% line.

Why it matters. The one report whose whole purpose is a percentage against a threshold is the one that omits the threshold. The reader cannot tell whether 90,84% — or a bundle’s 77,40% — passed. The same product answers “how did we do” three different ways.

Fix. Give V11 V5’s KPI row with “Schwelle: 90%” and pass/fail colouring, rename the column “Pünktlichkeit (%)”, and draw the 90% line in the chart.

H9

Run Now with a schedule ticked confirms only half the action

High
Wizard step 3 · UX ×3 + Trust + Clarity — 5 lenses

Ticking “Als geplanten Bericht einrichten”, choosing Monthly and pressing Run Now produces the toast “Evaluation started — running now”. Nothing mentions the schedule, and no new entry appears in Geplante Berichte.

ReproducedTicked the box, set Monthly, clicked Run Now, opened Geplante Berichte — only the six pre-existing demo schedules.

H10

Kantone is the only filter that closes when you pick something

High
Wizard step 2 · UX ×3

Of six visually identical multi-select panels, five stay open after a pick and Kantone closes.

ReproducedAll six in one pass: RPV, Verkehrsmittel, TU’s, Linien, Haltestellen → stays open; Kantone → CLOSES on pick. Adding a second canton by mouse then failed twice, because reopening lands on the centre-mounted ✕ (C1).

H13

A failed evaluation is a dead end

High
Evaluations list — 4 rows with a red Failed badge · UX round 2, conf 85

The badge is a <span class="badge badge-red"> with no title; row and cell onclick are both null; clicking it changes nothing (page text length identical before and after). The row’s only action is Delete.

Why it matters. The analyst is told it failed but not why or what to do, so the only move the UI offers is to destroy the evidence and rebuild the whole evaluation — without knowing whether it will fail the same way.

Fix. Make the badge open a short reason (“keine Daten für den gewählten Zeitraum”, “Import unvollständig”) and add a Retry action alongside delete.

H14

Three report views have no keyboard route back to the list

High
V5 Anschlusspünktlichkeit, V8 Fahrtausfälle, V10 Ausfallmaske · A11Y round 2, Critical, conf 92

The breadcrumb “Evaluations” is a <span> with onclick and tabIndex −1; the sidebar items are <div>s with tabIndex −1; all five topbar links are href="#" onclick="return false"; and unlike V11 and the raw view, these three ship no Close button. A keyboard user who opens one of them is stuck there.

Fix. Fix the breadcrumb and sidebar focusability (C5 covers both) and add the “Schließen” button V11 already has — which also resolves the consistency finding in the Medium list.

H15

Screen readers get no announcement on view change, and 60 row buttons announce as “bar_chart”

High
Every view transition · button.punct-act-btn / .rpt-act-btn · A11Y round 2, conf 88–90

Activating anything that swaps the view (Anzeigen → report, Raw data → table, Close → report) leaves document.activeElement on <body>, and there is no aria-live region or focus target — so nothing is announced. Separately, the row-level Chart and Raw-data buttons are named by their Material-icon ligature text, which overrides the title attribute: all 60 buttons on a report announce as either “bar_chart” or “table_chart”, with no row context.

Fix. Move focus to the new view’s heading on transition (or announce it in a live region). Give the icon spans aria-hidden="true" and the buttons a real aria-label including the row — “Diagramm für ARAG BusÜ 1”.

H16

The disabled Delete is mouse-blocked and keyboard-live

High
In-Progress rows — button.action-icon.danger · A11Y round 1, conf 95

Disabled only visually: pointer-events:none and opacity:.35, with no disabled attribute and no aria-disabled. Hit-testing the pixel returns div.action-icons, not the button — but it stays at tabIndex 0, and Enter fires the real confirm() and deletes the row.

Why it matters. The one destructive action in the tool is unreachable by the users it is meant to work for and fully live for the users it is meant to be blocked for.

Fix. Set the real disabled attribute (or aria-disabled plus a guard in the handler) and name it with the reason: “Löschen — nicht möglich, solange die Auswertung läuft”.

Medium — worth fixing, not urgent

  1. 37 translation keys exist in neither dictionary. data-i18n keys used in the markup that are missing from both the en and de dictionaries, so those elements never translate. They cluster in the Ausfallmaske (fa_mask_*, fa_col_*, fa_opt_*) and the raw punctuality columns (punct_raw_col_*, punct_col_ist, punct_col_delta).
  2. The breakdown dropdown is half-translated — a different fault. In EN mode five of eleven options stay German: Linienbündel, Haltestelle, Monat, TU konzessioniert, TU fahrend. Those five have no i18n key at all, so they are not among the 37 above. One dropdown reads “No selection, Operating day, Linienbündel, Line, Haltestelle, Monat, CW, TU konzessioniert, TU fahrend, Transport mode, Region”.
  3. “Transport companies” in English inside the German UI. The chip dropdown header on all three report views. Flagged in the June review as UX-1 and still open.
  4. Only V11 tells you when it was generated. #punct-eval-sub renders “Auswertung: Punctuality DPM (25.06.2026 22:54:42)”; V5 and V8 have nothing. For a scheduled report that may be days old there is no way to judge freshness. June review UX-4, still open.
  5. “Schließen” exists on two views out of six. Present on Pünktlichkeit and the raw punctuality view; absent on Anschlusspünktlichkeit, Fahrtausfälle and the Ausfallmaske. June review UX-3, still open — and now also an accessibility blocker (H14).
  6. Four acronyms carry the meaning and none is expanded. DPM, RPV, TU, DQI appear on the type cards and filter labels with no expansion or tooltip anywhere.
  7. Deleting uses the browser’s native confirm(). An unstyled OS dialog in a designed tool, and the toast afterwards offers no undo despite warning the action cannot be undone. The scheduled list uses a second, separate implementation of the same pattern.
  8. Two design languages in one file. 109 distinct hex values, 41 used exactly once, two grey ramps mixed (#9CA3AF/#E5E7EB/#374151 alongside #9E9E9E/#E0E0E0/#424242), 13 font sizes, 10 border radii. .rpt-params-bar and .fa-params-bar are byte-identical duplicates (June UX-7).
  9. Secondary text fails contrast. Declared text colours below 4.5:1 on white: #D1D5DB 1.47, #BDBDBD 1.88, #9CA3AF 2.54 (used 40×), #9E9E9E 2.68, #D97706 3.19.
  10. A reversed date range is accepted silently. From 20.08.2026 to 01.08.2026: no validation, no error, Next stays enabled, and the generated name reads “Trip Failures DPM – 20.08.2026 – 01.08.2026”.
  11. Login accepts empty credentials. Anmelden with both fields blank logs straight in. Acceptable as a prototype shortcut, but the screen shows no validation design at all.
  12. Step-3 summary concatenates the filters. “RPV: Tarifverbund ATransport Mode: Bahn NormalspurCantons: AGLines: Bus 10Stops: Aarau” — no separators between values on the confirmation card.
  13. The sidebar shows no location inside a report. No .nav-item is active while a report is open, so the navigation gives no sense of where you are.
  14. The bordered-field params style on V10 and V12 is intentional, not drift. Raw and detail views keep the field-column style while V5/V8/V11 use grey chips. Documented so a future review does not re-flag it.

What works, and should not be touched

  • The arithmetic holds up. On the Pünktlichkeit report Delta = Soll − Ist exactly and Wert % = pünktlich / Ist, on the Total row (2 101 488 / 2 048 996 / 1 861 401 / 52 492 / 90,84%) and on all five bundle rows spot-checked. Every figure is internally consistent — which is exactly why H1 and H2 are dangerous rather than obvious.
  • The wizard’s auto-naming is genuinely good. Every choice feeds the evaluation name live — “Trip Failures DPM – Current Month, all lines” becomes “…, Mon–Fri” the moment you untick Sat and Sun. The clearest feedback mechanism in the tool.
  • Landing on the evaluations list, not an empty form, is the right call. Grouped by report type, with search, status and period filters that all apply instantly and a real empty state for no-match searches.
  • Pagination on 4 950 rows works properly — First/Previous/1/2/…/495/Next/Last, with the “1 to 10 of 4950” line updating correctly.
  • The custom-period rule shipped this week holds. Selecting a custom range disables scheduling with a stated reason, clears an already-ticked box, and blocks the edit path — verified in both languages.
  • Chart zoom and reset work (svg width 1044 → 1566 → back), and the Ausfallmaske request does what it promises: the icon becomes a check and a data-sheet link appears.
  • KPI cards with thresholds are the strongest piece of design in the tool — “All day 89,53% ⚠ · Threshold: 90%” answers the question before you read a table row. Which is exactly why Pünktlichkeit not having them stands out (H8).

Suggested order

  1. C1 — the filter ✕. One CSS change, and it is currently destroying user input.
  2. H6 — first click on a bundle row. One class in the markup, plus the caret glyph.
  3. H1 + H3 — stop the report misstating its own contents. A truncation row and a period read from one source. Small, and they are the findings that could put a wrong number in front of the BAV.
  4. H12 — the six unwired buttons. Copy the handlers that already exist on sibling views.
  5. H2 — a “Demodaten” badge. One chip, and it neutralises the worst consequence of every data finding until there is a backend.
  6. C5 — the keyboard route in. Buttons for the type cards, role="combobox" on the six filter triggers, real nav elements in the sidebar. Mechanical, and without it the tool is unusable without a mouse.
  7. C3 — open finished evaluations. Needs a decision from you: do Rohdaten / Linienanalyse / DQI get report views, or a download action?
  8. C4 + H5 — breakdown selects and column filters. The largest piece of real work, and what makes the reports usable rather than illustrative.
  9. C2 — hydrate the edit-schedule wizard, or rename that screen until it does.
  10. H4, H7, H8 — header truth and the missing threshold. Period from the stored window, chips that admit they are read-only, KPI cards on V11.
  11. C6, H14, H15, H16 — the rest of the accessibility set. Modal semantics, a Close button on three views, focus on view change, a real disabled on Delete.

Known gaps in this review

Recorded by the validators themselves so that “not checked” never reads as “passed”.

Never reached at all

  1. Rohdaten Export, Linienanalyse DPM and Datenqualitätsindex DPM have no report view, so three of the five evaluation types were not assessed for reading at all — only for the missing open action (C3).
  2. The three full-page chart views (view-chart-punct, -connection, -fa) were never opened — the row Chart button opens the popup instead. Their behaviour and keyboard handling are unknown.
  3. view-raw-connection was not exercised; it is assumed structurally identical to the punctuality raw view, and its paginator and return path are unverified.
  4. No loading, error or empty-result state exists anywhere in either flow. Login accepts empty fields, the reversed date range raises nothing, reports render instantly from in-page data. So error copy, recovery and progress feedback are entirely unexamined — and for a tool that will fetch real BAV data, that is the largest untested surface.
  5. The first-run empty states of the evaluations list and Geplante Berichte were never reached.

Partially checked

  1. No screen reader was actually run. No NVDA, VoiceOver or Orca available — every announcement claim is computed from the browser’s ARIA tree and DOM semantics, not heard. Wording will differ by AT.
  2. The notify-by-email toggle and address field were never exercised. A control that promises a notification is exactly where an unkept promise hides.
  3. Whether completing the edit-schedule wizard overwrites, duplicates or discards was not tested — C2’s data-loss consequence is inferred from the blank form, not observed.
  4. Whether filter cascading is intended at all is unknown, so it is unclear whether the Linien picker can offer lines outside the selected cantons, or whether an impossible combination can be committed.
  5. Enter/Space activation of the type cards could not be tested — they are not focusable, so only the absence of any keyboard route is proven.
  6. V5 and V8 were only read in EN mode this round, so whether their chips and thresholds read cleanly in German is unverified.
  7. Whether the title/period contradiction (H4) is demo data or systematic is unknown — no evaluation was opened whose stored period actually matched the last 30 days relative to its generation date.
