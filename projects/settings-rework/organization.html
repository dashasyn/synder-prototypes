Billing questions
What transactions am I charged for?

Any transaction Synder syncs — sales receipts, refunds, payouts. Processing fees are never charged, though they are still synced to your books.

Why can't I change currency when resubscribing?

Currency is locked for the paid period. When the cycle ends a switcher appears and you can resubscribe in another one.

Will my discount remain if I change plan?

Yes — discounts attach to your account, not the plan, and re-apply automatically.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are final and non-refundable under our Terms. That's why a mid-term downgrade shows what you'd lose and offers to schedule the change for your renewal date instead.

Are there discounts for me?

Discounts unlock the longer you stay. Anything you've earned shows as a line in your breakdown.

Is tax included?

No. VAT/GST/sales tax is added at checkout from your billing address and itemised on the invoice.

How is a mid-cycle change charged?

Whatever's left on your current plan is credited against the new charge. On a downgrade the credit can exceed the new term — the review step shows that before you confirm.
