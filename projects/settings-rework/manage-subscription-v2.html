Billing questions

The four answers that describe rules the interface should state are also shown inline where they apply — what counts as a transaction sits on the transactions row, tax sits under the total, discount persistence sits in the breakdown, and the currency lock sits next to the coupon field.

What transactions am I charged for?

Any transaction Synder syncs — sales receipts, refunds, payouts. Processing fees are never charged and never count against your monthly balance.

Why can't I change currency when resubscribing?

Currency is locked for the paid period. Once the billing cycle ends a currency switcher appears and you can resubscribe in another one.

Will my discount remain if I update the plan?

Yes. Discounts attach to your account rather than the plan and are re-applied automatically. Changing the card doesn't affect them either.

Can I get a refund for the subscription?

Under our Terms a purchase is final and non-refundable. That's why a mid-term downgrade shows you the balance you'd lose and offers to schedule the change for your renewal date instead.

Are there any discounts available for me?

Discounts unlock the longer you stay with us. Anything you've already earned appears as a line in the breakdown under "What you're paying for".

Is tax included in the subscription price?

No. VAT, GST or sales tax is added at checkout based on the billing address on your organization, and itemised on the invoice.

How does a mid-cycle change get charged?

Whatever's left on your current plan is credited against the new charge, so you only pay the difference. On a downgrade the credit can exceed the new term — we show you that before you confirm.

Need a special plan?

Higher volumes, unusual integrations or bespoke automation — we'll put one together and quote it.
