Billing questions
What transactions am I charged for?

Synder charges for syncing any transaction type — sales receipts, refunds, payouts — except processing fees. Processing fees never count against your monthly balance.

Why can't I change currency when resubscribing?

Currency is fixed for the duration of a paid period. Once the billing cycle ends you'll see a currency switcher and can subscribe in another currency.

Will my discount survive a plan change?

Yes. Discounts follow your account, not the plan, and are re-applied automatically. Changing the card doesn't affect them either.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are final and non-refundable under our Terms, but contact support and they'll look at your case.

Are there discounts for me?

Discounts unlock with account age — the longer you stay, the cheaper it gets. Any you've unlocked already show in the breakdown above.

Is tax included?

No. VAT/GST/sales tax is added on top based on the billing address in Organization details, calculated at checkout and itemized on the invoice.

How do I change my subscription?

Use Change plan & add-ons above. Anything left on your current balance is credited against the new charge automatically.
