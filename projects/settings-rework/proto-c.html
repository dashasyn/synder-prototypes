Common questions

What transactions am I charged for?

Any transaction Synder syncs — sales receipts, refunds, payouts. Processing fees are never charged and never count against your monthly balance.

Why can't I change currency when resubscribing?

Currency is locked for the paid period. Once the cycle ends, a currency switcher appears and you can resubscribe in another one.

Will my discount remain if I update the plan?

Yes — discounts attach to your account, not the plan, and re-apply automatically. Changing the card doesn't affect them.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are final and non-refundable under our Terms. Contact support if your case is unusual.

Are there any discounts for me?

Discounts unlock the longer you stay. Anything you've earned already shows in the breakdown under Payment & invoices.

Is tax included in the price?

No. VAT/GST/sales tax is added at checkout based on your billing address and itemized on the invoice.

How does a mid-term change get charged?

Whatever's left on your current plan is credited against the new charge, so you only pay the difference.

Cancel subscription

Your plan stays active until 27 Jul 2027. After that syncing stops and your data becomes read-only.
