Billing questions

What transactions am I charged for?

Any transaction Synder syncs — sales receipts, refunds, payouts. Processing fees are never charged and never touch your monthly balance.

Why can't I change currency when resubscribing?

Currency is locked for the paid period. When the cycle ends, a currency switcher appears and you can resubscribe in another one.

Will my discount remain if I update the plan?

Yes — discounts attach to your account, not the plan, and re-apply automatically. Changing the card doesn't affect them.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are final and non-refundable under our Terms. Contact support if your situation is unusual.

Are there any discounts for me?

Discounts unlock the longer you stay with us. Anything you've earned already appears in the total breakdown.

Is tax included in the price?

No. VAT/GST/sales tax is added at checkout based on the billing address in Organization → Details, and itemized on your invoice.

How does the switch get charged?

Whatever's left on your current plan is credited against the new charge, so you only pay the difference.
