Who we work with

🚆

Private Railways

Regional and intercity rail operators across Germany, Austria, and Switzerland. Our ITCS DatNet platform runs on more than 80% of German private railway networks, making it the de facto industry standard.

  • Real-time dispatch and operations management
  • Timetable data processing and distribution
  • Passenger information on platforms and in-vehicle
  • VDV interface compliance and data exchange with Deutsche Bahn

Bus & Urban Transit

City and regional bus operators managing complex multi-route networks with demanding operational requirements.

  • ITCS for bus fleet management and routing
  • Driver information systems and digital cockpits
  • Real-time passenger announcements and displays
  • Stop management and accessibility compliance

Transport Authorities

Regional transport authorities (Aufgabenträger) and planning organizations responsible for ordering and monitoring contracted services.

  • Q.Daba quality measurement and reporting
  • KPI dashboards for contracted service performance
  • Regulatory compliance documentation
  • Cross-operator data aggregation and benchmarking

Vehicle Manufacturers

OEMs and system integrators who need to embed transport control interfaces into rolling stock — buses, trams, and rail vehicles.

  • Onboard computing and RAILTAB driver tablets
  • Integration with ticketing and validation systems
  • Real-time data connectivity to control centers
  • Certified VDV-compliant software modules

Q.Daba: The Quality Standard for Swiss Public Transport

Switzerland's Federal Office of Transport (BAV) selected Q.Daba as the official platform for nationwide quality measurement of Regional Passenger Transport (RPV). Today, every major Swiss transport operator reports through our system.

For transport authorities, Q.Daba means consistent data, defensible KPIs, and clear accountability — across every operator in the network.
