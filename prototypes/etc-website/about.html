25 Years Building the Future of Public Transport

From a small railway engineering firm to Europe's leading public transport digitalization specialist — ETC Solutions has been at the forefront of ÖPNV innovation since 1999.

Our Story

ETC Solutions began as a classical railway engineering consultancy. Over two decades, our small IT division grew into a full-scale software house — today, fully digitalized, paperless, and operating at the frontier of AI-driven transport intelligence.

We are what we call 'industry digitalizers' — partners to transport companies, authorities, and vehicle manufacturers who want to make public transport smarter, more reliable, and more passenger-friendly.

  • Founded 1999 in Berlin
  • Expanded to Switzerland (Olten) in 2011
  • 80%+ of German private railways run on our ITCS platform
  • Official technology partner of Swiss Federal Office of Transport (BAV)
