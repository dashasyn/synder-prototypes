Measure what matters

Q.Daba captures quality measurements across your entire service portfolio — on-time performance, passenger information accuracy, vehicle condition, cleanliness, and more. All in one place, all in real time.

  • Automated measurement collection from multiple sources
  • Configurable KPI frameworks per line, operator, or authority
  • Real-time and historical dashboards
  • Regulatory report generation (PDF/Excel export)

Key Capabilities

A complete quality intelligence platform

📈

Performance Dashboards

Live and historical views of quality KPIs across your network. Drill down by line, vehicle type, time period, or service category.

🏛️

Regulatory Reporting

Generate structured reports for transport authorities and BAV submissions. Pre-built templates for Swiss RPV, German SPNV, and Austrian ÖPNV frameworks.

🔗

Data Integration

Connects to ITCS systems, GPS logs, ticketing data, and manual inspection inputs. One unified quality record per service event.

🔍

Measurement Audit Trail

Every quality data point is traceable — source, timestamp, method. Full defensibility for regulatory audits and contract disputes.

⚖️

Contractor Benchmarking

Compare quality performance across multiple operators or contract lots. Essential for transport authorities managing competitive tendering.

🤖

Anomaly Detection

AI-assisted flagging of unusual quality patterns — helping quality managers focus attention where it's most needed.
