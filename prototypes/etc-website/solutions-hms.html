The problem with stop data today

In most transport networks, stop data is fragmented across multiple systems and organizations — operators, authorities, infrastructure managers, and information providers each maintain their own incomplete version. The result: inconsistencies in passenger information, timetable errors, and costly manual reconciliation.

  • Multiple conflicting stop records across systems
  • Manual updates that never fully propagate
  • Passenger display errors from stale data
  • Integration failures at network boundaries
